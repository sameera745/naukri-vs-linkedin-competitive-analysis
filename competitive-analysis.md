If I were the Product Manager at Naukri, I would build a Career Signal feature.
The feature would allow professionals to privately indicate their job-search status, such as:
Open to opportunities
Curious about opportunities
Not looking
The status could automatically expire or update over time, reducing the need for users to repeatedly maintain their profiles. Recruiters could see relevant availability signals while searching Naukri's recruiter database.
This would address one of Naukri's major strategic gaps: keeping experienced professionals engaged even when they are not actively searching for a job.
Instead of trying to copy LinkedIn's social feed, Naukri could focus on its existing strength in job discovery and recruitment while making passive-candidate visibility easier.
Key Strategic Insight
Naukri is stronger as a job-search and recruitment platform, while LinkedIn is stronger as a professional networking and personal-branding platform.
The opportunity for Naukri is therefore not to become another LinkedIn, but to make its existing recruitment platform more useful to passive candidates and experienced professionals.

Step 5 — Strategic Recommendation
Naukri's Next Big Product Bet: Career Signal
Naukri's core weakness isn't its resume database; it is that users primarily return when they are actively looking for a job. LinkedIn attracts passive, mid-to-senior professionals by giving them reasons to stay engaged through professional identity, content, networking, and visibility.
The recommended product bet is "Career Signal" — a lightweight, privacy-first status layer where users can indicate their current career interest, such as "Open," "Curious," or "Not looking." The status would remain active and automatically expire or change over time. Recruiters could see relevant availability and intent signals, while candidates could remain discoverable without constantly updating their profiles.
This would address an important gap between the two platforms: Naukri is strong in job search and recruitment, while LinkedIn is stronger in professional networking and passive-candidate engagement. Career Signal would help Naukri retain experienced professionals and make its platform useful even when users are not actively searching for jobs.
The feature should also include strong privacy controls, such as hiding the status from a current employer or limiting visibility to recruiters.
Rather than copying LinkedIn's social feed, Naukri should strengthen its existing recruitment ecosystem while making passive-candidate engagement easier.
Step 6 — Proposed Product Feature: Career Signal
6.1 Problem Statement
Naukri is highly effective for users who are actively searching for jobs, but it has less emphasis on keeping passive candidates engaged and discoverable when they are not actively applying.
Professionals who are open to new opportunities may have little reason to regularly revisit or update their Naukri profile. This can make it harder for recruiters to identify candidates who are currently interested in opportunities.
LinkedIn addresses part of this problem through professional identity, networking, and visibility.
6.2 Product Opportunity
Naukri can address this gap without trying to become a social-networking platform.
The proposed Career Signal feature would allow candidates to communicate their current career interest through a simple status:
🟢 Open to Opportunities
🟡 Curious About Opportunities
⚪ Not Looking
The status would be visible to relevant recruiters according to the candidate's privacy settings and could automatically expire or change over time.
6.3 Product Goal
Make it easier for recruiters to identify relevant candidates who are open to opportunities while giving professionals more control over how and when they are discovered.
6.4 Key Value
For Candidates:
Communicate career interest without actively applying for jobs.
Control who can see their career status.
For Recruiters:
Identify candidates who are more likely to respond.
Prioritize relevant candidates based on their current career interest.
6.5 Privacy Considerations
Because passive candidates may already be employed, Career Signal should provide strong privacy controls.
Possible controls include:
Hide Career Signal from current employer.
Show the signal only to recruiters.
Allow candidates to turn the signal off at any time.
Automatically expire the status after a defined period.
6.6 Expected Product Impact
Career Signal could help Naukri:
Increase engagement from passive candidates.
Improve recruiter-to-candidate discovery.
Make candidate profiles more actionable.
Strengthen Naukri's position against LinkedIn without copying its social-networking model.
6.7 Success Metrics
The feature should be evaluated using measurable product metrics:
Career Signal activation rate
Passive-candidate retention
Recruiter-to-candidate contact rate
Candidate response rate
Profile reactivation rate
Percentage of recruiters using Career Signal as a search/filter signal
